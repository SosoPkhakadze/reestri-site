Armenian costs, with receipts

For Armenia, state fees are usually about USD 8 to 35 per document and the local law firm about USD 70 per company. A full Armenian set normally adds about USD 100 to 135 per company. Other scopes are quoted individually.

The law firm's part does not exceed USD 150 per company without your approval. An available expedited service is quoted separately and carries no guarantee. You receive receipts and the reconciliation of any advance.

Delivery timing

Georgian extracts normally take one working day. Armenian extracts are usually available the same or next working day after filing. Charters and file copies depend on the Register: often 1 to 2 working days with an available expedited service, or up to 7 without. The written quote confirms timing. Expedited processing is not guaranteed.

Payment, advances and cancellation

There is no payment when you send a request. We confirm the scope, fee, cost cap, timing and payment terms in writing first.

For new clients, small fixed-scope orders are paid upfront after quote approval. Larger matters normally require 50% of the service fee plus the approved external-cost advance before work starts. The remaining service fee is due before final delivery.

Unused cost advances are refunded. The quote explains cancellation and what happens if a record is unavailable. Established clients may have separately agreed invoice terms. Payment is by bank transfer in USD.

What is not included?

Armenian CJSC/OJSC shareholder lists, private court files, field visits and certified translations are not included. Read the full limits.
