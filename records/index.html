What is not included?

Armenian joint-stock shareholder lists and private court files are not available through the records desk. An English summary is not a certified translation. See coverage and limitations.

Timing and availability

Georgian extracts normally take one working day. Armenian extracts are usually available the same or next working day after filing. Charters and file copies depend on the Register, often 1 to 2 working days with an available expedited service, or up to 7 without. Your written quote confirms timing. Expedited processing is not guaranteed.

Where should we reply?

We will send your reference and the next steps by email. No account is needed.
