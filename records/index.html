Costs, at cost

State fees of about USD 8 to 35 per document, and the local law firm that files for us, about USD 70 per company. For the full Armenian set that comes to USD 100 to 135 per company. A receipt for every item.

Never above the cap

The law firm's part never goes above USD 150 per company without your approval. The Register's expedited service, when its counter offers it, is about USD 29 per company and carries no guarantee. We ask before using it.

What you receive

Electronic documents in PDF, as the registers issue them, plus the English summary. The Armenian extract carries a control number anyone can check on the government verification site. Certified paper copies or an apostille are quoted separately.

Limits

What we cannot get, so nobody is surprised

  • Shareholders of Armenian joint-stock companies (CJSC, OJSC)The State Register does not hold them. The Central Depository does, and it releases them only to the company or its shareholders. Declared beneficial owners are public, and we include them.
  • Full court filesOpen to the parties only. Published decisions and hearing lists are public, and that is what the court-case check covers.
  • Site visits, interviewsWe order official records only. Nothing that is not in a register.
  • Certified translationsDocuments come as issued, in Armenian or Georgian, with an English summary. A certified translation can be quoted separately.

How it works

From your order to the documents

  1. Send the order below, or email soso@reestri.io with the company names and registration numbers.
  2. We confirm scope, fee and a date in writing, usually the same working day (Georgia time, GMT+4). Nothing is ordered or spent until you agree. Your NDA is fine: attach it to the order.
  3. Our local representative files with the Register. Every company and named person is checked against UK, EU and US sanctions lists first. Documents go to you as they arrive, each with its English summary.
  4. One invoice when delivery ends: the fee, plus costs with a receipt for each item. Payment by bank transfer in USD.
