Across the three

Four rules that hold in every register

  • The identifier beats the nameGeorgian 9-digit code, Armenian 8-digit tax ID, Moldovan 13-digit IDNO. All three are printed on documents and all three search cleanly; names are ambiguous in three scripts and two encodings.
  • Status words do not travel"Registered" in Georgia can mean dormant for a decade; Armenia's status is a sentence about liquidation; Moldova has no status column at all and you derive it from a liquidation date. Record the original wording, not your translation.
  • Ownership costs differentlyPublic and self-declared in Armenia, public in the Moldovan state file, paid extract in Georgia. Budget for the country you are in.
  • Keep the evidenceSave the page or the file, the URL and the date. Registers change weekly and the version you saw may not be reproducible next month.
