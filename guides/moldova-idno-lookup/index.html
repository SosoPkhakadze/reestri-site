How to check a Moldovan company by IDNO

Moldova publishes its whole company register as an open file every Monday, founders and share sizes included. Most people never find it and pay a mirror site instead. This guide covers the 13-digit IDNO, the state's own lookup and the free mirrors, the weekly dataset, the certified extract and its exact tariff, the diacritics trap, and what stays private.

By Soso Pkhakadze · published 5 Sep 2026 · portal facts checked 5 Sep 2026 · Disclosure: I build and sell Reestri, the paid tool named in the last section. Every step above it works without it.

Step one

Get the 13-digit IDNO

Every legal entity in Moldova has an IDNO (Identificatorul de stat al unității de drept): 13 digits, for example 1003602023531. It doubles as the fiscal code (cod fiscal), so there is one number for the register, the tax authority, customs and the banks. Moldovan law requires it on invoices and contracts, and most shops print it in the website footer next to the SRL name.

Search by IDNO whenever you have one. Company names in the register are stored in Romanian with inconsistent diacritics (more on that below), older records may be in Russian, and popular words repeat across hundreds of SRLs. The IDNO resolves to exactly one record. About 37,000 of the oldest entries in the state file, mostly registrations from the 1990s, carry a short pre-IDNO code or none; a company trading today without a 13-digit IDNO is unusual enough to ask about.

Step two

Three free ways to look one up

  1. The state's own lookup: date.gov.md/open/company-details (switch to ?culture=en for English). Type the IDNO, pass the reCAPTCHA, and read "Date de bază" (basic data) plus a field the open file does not have: debts to the state budget. One company at a time, and the captcha is the state's way of saying so; do not script it.
  2. The weekly open dataset on dataset.gov.md: one spreadsheet with every registered enterprise, directors and founders included. Download, open, filter on the IDNO column. It is the primary source for everything in the next section.
  3. The mirrors: openmoney.md and idno.md republish the same dataset with a search box. Convenient, but third-party: check the snapshot date they show, and for anything you will cite, confirm against the state file or the state lookup.

The Public Services Agency (ASP, Agenția Servicii Publice) keeps the register itself; its website at asp.gov.md describes the services and tariffs but has no search of its own.

The primary source

The weekly open dataset: 302,232 companies, founders and shares included

The dataset is titled "Date din Registrul de stat al unităților de drept privind întreprinderile înregistrate în Republica Moldova" and published by ASP on the national open-data portal. A new XLSX file, about 38 MB, appears every Monday; the file of 24 Aug 2026 holds 302,232 rows on its "Company" sheet, one per enterprise, plus two sheets with the activity-code dictionaries. The archive goes back to December 2014, monthly at first and weekly since 2019, and every old file is still downloadable, which makes it a free change history: diff two Mondays and you see new registrations, liquidations and every change of director or founder in between. A sister dataset covers non-commercial organisations.

There is no status column. "Active" is derived: liquidation date empty. Suspension and insolvency states are not in the file (they are in the captcha-gated state lookup and in the paid information services below), and a "Lichidator" among the directors is your hint that a liquidation has started. A beneficial-owners column appears in some years' files and not in others; the 24 Aug 2026 file has eleven columns and no such column. Legal-form shorthand: SRL limited liability company, SA joint-stock company, II individual enterprise, GT farming household, IS state enterprise.

The official document

The certified extract: 110 to 440 lei, by speed

When a bank, a court or a foreign registry wants proof rather than a spreadsheet row, ASP issues an Extras din Registrul de stat al persoanelor juridice: a stamped document with the company's registration data, current administrator and founders as of the issue date. Anyone can order one for any company. Order online through the state services portal (servicii.gov.md, which now forwards to evo.gov.md) or at any ASP office; the fee depends only on how fast you want it.

The history document is the one to order when the question is "who owned this before 2024", since the open file only ever shows the current founders.

Alphabet

The diacritics trap, and other name problems

Romanian has five letters with diacritics: ă, â, î, ș, ț. Moldovan records were typed over three decades on keyboards that produced two different encodings for two of them: ș and ț with a comma below (the correct Romanian letters) and ş and ţ with a cedilla (the Turkish letters that older Windows keyboards offered). To a search box they are different characters. A name typed with commas will not match a record stored with cedillas, and the same company can appear both ways across years of files. Many records also drop the diacritics entirely and shout in capitals: CHISINAU, RISCANI.

Practical habits: search the IDNO and skip the problem; if you must search a name, try it without any diacritics first, then with commas, then with cedillas; and copy the exact string from a record rather than retyping it. Foreign founders appear as they were transliterated at registration (a German founder can be spelled the Russian way), and pre-2000 records may carry Russian names, so a person search needs the same patience. The tool below folds all of these forms to one before matching, which is most of what it adds over a spreadsheet filter.

The honest map

What is not public in Moldova

  • Personal numbersDirectors' and founders' IDNP (the 13-digit personal number) is not in the open file and not on the free lookup. Names and roles are; nothing else about the people.
  • Home addressesNot published. The address in the file is the company's registered seat.
  • Suspension, insolvency, court administratorsNot in the open file. The state lookup shows some of it; ASP sells the rest as separate information services (73 to 109 lei each).
  • Tax debtsOnly the captcha-gated state lookup shows "restanțe față de bugetul de stat", debts to the state budget, and only one company at a time.
  • Ownership historyThe current file shows current founders. History means either diffing the archived weekly files yourself or ordering ASP's history document (245 lei).
  • Re-use termsThe dataset's licence field on the portal reads "not specified". The data is published by the state as public information; what a commercial re-publisher may do with it has not been confirmed in writing, which is why our own Moldova tool is opt-in. See below.
