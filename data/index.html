Armenia cut single-source contracts from 52 percent of spend to 7. The habit stayed.

236,159 signed contracts from PPCM, Armenia's procurement registry, 2018 to 2026. The value share of single-source awards fell from 52.04 to 6.83 percent while roughly four procedures in ten still skip competition. The Ministry of Health signed 486.7 billion AMD without tenders, 1,080 companies get over 90 percent of their public revenue from one buyer, and appeals fell from 142 contracts a year to 18.

story 2

The single-source money left the Ministry of Health. Here is where it went.

The Ministry of Health's single-source share of its own spend fell from 95.70 percent in 2018 to 5.98 in 2026. The largest single-source buyers of 2024 to 2026 are now the Council of Public Broadcaster, Yerevan Municipality, the Ministry of High-Tech Industry and the Probation Service. The supplier base grew from 3,072 winners to 7,795, over a third of each year's winners are new to the registry, and the median contract shrank while billion-AMD contracts multiplied.
