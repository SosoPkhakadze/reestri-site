Your request details.

What we keep, why, and how to contact us about it.

Who handles your request

Reestri is run by Soso Pkhakadze, Kutaisi, Georgia. Contact soso@reestri.io about your information.

What the form collects

Your firm, contact name, email, selected country, company names and identifiers, requested documents, optional deadline and notes, and any NDA you attach. We use them to answer and run your request.

Only include the information needed for your request. An uploaded NDA is for review; uploading it does not mean an agreement has been accepted.

Where it is handled

The order store uses Cloudflare. Request emails are delivered to Reestri's Google mailbox; confirmation emails use Resend. Cloudflare Turnstile checks form submissions for abuse. Relevant company and scope details may need to be provided to the registry or local representative to carry out an agreed order. We confirm the scope and any confidentiality requirements before ordering.

We do not sell your request information or use it for unrelated marketing.

Retention

Attached NDAs are deleted from the Cloudflare order store after 90 days; order details are removed from that store after 24 months. These automatic limits apply to the order store, not every copy in email or matter files. Contact Soso to discuss access, correction, deletion and any copies needed for an existing matter or invoicing.

Data-tool queries and website visits

Apify tool runs use your own Apify account. The website's live lookup sends the query to Reestri's demo API, which has rate limits and can cache results for up to six hours. Do not put confidential matter details into that public lookup.

The site sends a simple page-view count with the page path and referring hostname. It does not add an analytics cookie or browser account. Infrastructure providers may keep operational logs under their own policies.

Your questions

To ask what Reestri holds, correct a request or request deletion, email soso@reestri.io from the address you used. We may verify the request before sharing private information.
