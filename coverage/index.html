Sources, scope and clear limits.

Understand what a record or search can tell you before relying on it.

Armenian shareholder lists

The State Register does not hold shareholder lists for CJSCs and OJSCs. The Central Depository releases them only to the company or its shareholders. Public beneficial-owner declarations may be available; they are not a complete shareholder register.

Public court records only

A court-case check covers public hearing lists, published decisions and case information. Full court files are available only to the parties.

Documents and language

Electronic PDFs come from the original register, with an English summary of key facts. A summary is not a certified translation. Certified translations, certified paper copies and apostilles are quoted separately if available.

Records, not field investigation

The records desk orders official documents from Armenia and Georgia. It does not include site visits or interviews. Moldova guides and data tools are separate self-service research routes.

Where the records come from

  • Armenia: State Register Agency, Ministry of Justice. Public beneficial-ownership declarations and public court information are separate sources.
  • Georgia: National Agency of Public Registry (NAPR).

We keep the issuer and available verification details with the delivered records. Public declarations are what the company has filed, not an independent guarantee of ownership.
