Method

Rules the tools never break

  • Official sources onlyNAPR, e‑register.moj.am, armeps.am, dataset.gov.md. Open surfaces; no captcha bypass, no login walls, no robots‑excluded paths. Georgia's gated ownership extracts stay gated; we say so instead of pretending.
  • Evidence on every recordSource URL, retrieval timestamp and SHA‑256 of the fetched content. To audit a record, fetch its sourceUrl and recompute the hash: it matches, or the register changed after we read it, and the timestamp says when.
  • No personal identifiersOwners and directors are named as the state publishes them; personal ID numbers and home addresses are never returned.
  • Your queries stay yoursLookups run inside your own Apify account. We see aggregate usage statistics, never your inputs or results. Nothing to install, nothing of yours stored here; this page sets no cookies.
  • Honest failureRegistry down or changed? The run fails loudly and the record says unavailable. Daily scheduled checks keep the public success stats real; a red day is visible, not hidden.
  • CorrectionsRegistry data belongs to its registries; errors in it are theirs to fix, and each record's sourceUrl points at the authority. Errors in our parsing are ours: write to soso@reestri.io and broken records are fixed or refunded.

About

One person, on the ground, accountable

Reestri is built and run by Soso Pkhakadze, a software developer in Kutaisi, Georgia. The registers of this region are public in law and hard to use in practice: three scripts, card‑by‑card portals, no APIs. This is the missing data layer, built openly: the analyses are on GitHub, the servers are in public MCP registries, the tools carry their evidence.

Write in English, Georgian or Russian: soso@reestri.io. Journalists and researchers: lookups are free on request, send names.
